How to interpret the plot correctly
Here’s the right mental model:
• Grey points = all possible candidates
• Lime points = candidates the SVM thinks are worth exploring
• Red/blue background = SVM confidence, not objective value
• Black line = SVM decision boundary
• Concentric circles = SVM level sets, not optima
• The optimum = chosen later by EI/PI/UCB on the filtered grid
So the center of the circles is not the optimum — it’s just where the SVM’s geometry happens to be symmetric.
